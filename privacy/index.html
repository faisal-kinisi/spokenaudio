Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective [effective]

In short

SpokenAudio collects no personal information. Your recordings, transcripts and edits are kept on your iPhone, encrypted, and we never receive them. We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking.

1. Who we are

Create Simple Code LLC (“we”) makes SpokenAudio. Contact: support@spokenaudio.com · [address].

2. What stays on your iPhone

Audio, transcripts, the modification record, recording names, your Profile name and signature, settings, and the App’s activity log. Audio, transcripts and the modification record are encrypted with AES-256 on the device. Recording names are not yet encrypted. We cannot see, access or recover any of it.

3. What we collect

Nothing. The App has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting of its own and no tracking. It contains no code that sends your data to us.

4. Your voice and biometrics

We do not collect, create, store or use voiceprints, face geometry or any other biometric identifier or biometric information. The App does not analyze your voice to identify you or anyone else. Audio is used only so you can record, transcribe, play, edit and export it on your device, where it is kept as your own file until you delete it. Speech is transcribed on the device by Apple’s on-device speech recognition. The recording effects use only the loudness of the sound. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS; the App only receives “passed” or “not passed”.

5. Apple

Apple processes your purchase, trial and restore under Apple’s privacy policy; we receive no payment details. iOS downloads Apple’s speech model once. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, the backup includes SpokenAudio’s encrypted files; the key that opens them works only on this iPhone.

6. What you choose to share

When you export or share (files, passwords, the app log, or email to support), you decide where it goes. Once it leaves the App, its handling is governed by you, the recipient, and the services you use, not by us. The app log contains numbers and short codes, not your words or recording names, and you review it before sending. Please never send patient or client information to support.

7. Your rights (California, EU/UK and elsewhere)

We don’t sell or share personal information, and we hold none about you to access, correct or delete. Everything the App stores is on your device; deleting a recording, using Destroy keys, or deleting the App removes it. Questions: support@spokenaudio.com.

8. Children

SpokenAudio is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. We knowingly collect no information from anyone.

9. Changes

We’ll post any change here with a new version and date, and show material changes in the App.
