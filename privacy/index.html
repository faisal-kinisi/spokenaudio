Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective November 15, 2026

In short

The SpokenAudio app collects no personal information. Your recordings, transcripts and edits are kept on your iPhone, encrypted, and we never receive them. We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking. The only personal information we ever receive is what you choose to send us by email to support.

1. Who we are

Create Simple Code LLC (“we”), a California limited liability company, makes SpokenAudio. Contact: support@spokenaudio.com · 20660 Stevens Creek Blvd, Suite 276, Cupertino, CA 95014, United States · +1 (415) 508-8529.

2. What stays on your iPhone

Audio, transcripts, the modification record, recording names, your Profile name and signature, settings, and the App’s activity log. Audio, transcripts and the modification record are encrypted with AES-256 on the device. Not encrypted: recording names, dates, lengths and word counts, your Profile name and signature, settings, the app log, and audio you export without a password (including Export draft audio and copies rescued by earlier versions) until it is shared or deleted — so don’t put patient or client names in recording names. We cannot see, access or recover any of it.

3. What we collect

The App collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no automatic crash reporting and no tracking, and it contains no code that sends your data to us. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to email to support (Section 6).

4. Your voice and biometrics

We do not collect, create, store or use voiceprints, face geometry or any other biometric identifier or biometric information. The App does not analyze your voice to identify you or anyone else. Audio is used only so you can record, transcribe, play, edit and export it on your device, where it is kept as your own file until you delete it. Speech is transcribed on the device by Apple’s on-device speech recognition. The recording effects use only the loudness of the sound. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS; the App only receives “passed” or “not passed”.

5. Apple

Apple processes your purchase, trial and restore under Apple’s privacy policy; we receive no payment details. iOS downloads Apple’s speech model once. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, the backup includes SpokenAudio’s files: recordings, transcripts and the modification record stay encrypted, and the key that opens them works only on this iPhone; recording names, your Profile and settings are included as they are.

6. What you choose to share

When you export or share (files, passwords or the app log), you decide where it goes. Once it leaves the App, its handling is governed by you, the recipient, and the services you use, not by us. The app log holds times, settings and counts — for example when recordings started and stopped, errors and lock events — never your words, recording names or device names.

When you email us (for support, or to opt out of arbitration), we receive your email address, any name it shows, your message and any attachment, such as the app log. We use them only to reply to you, fix problems and keep the record of an opt-out (in the EU and UK, our legal basis is taking steps you ask for and our legitimate interest in supporting you and in keeping that record: GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (f)). Email reaches us through Cloudflare Email Routing (Cloudflare, Inc.), which forwards it without storing it, and is kept in our email service, Apple iCloud Mail (Apple Inc.). Both are US companies; Apple may keep iCloud data on its own or its providers’ servers. Support emails are deleted 1 year after your question is resolved; an arbitration opt-out is kept for as long as these Terms apply to you, as the record of your choice. We never sell or share them or use them for marketing. Please never send patient, client or other sensitive information to support.

7. Your rights (California, EU/UK and elsewhere)

We don’t sell or share personal information. Unless you have emailed support, we hold no personal information about you. If you have, you may ask to access, correct, delete or receive your support messages, or object to our use of them, at support@spokenaudio.com; in the EU or UK you may also complain to your data-protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office). Everything the App stores is on your device; deleting a recording, using Destroy keys, or deleting the App removes it from this iPhone. Backups made earlier may still contain it until they are replaced or deleted, and the encryption key, a saved export password and the trial status can remain in the iPhone’s Keychain until the iPhone is erased — use Destroy all encryption keys before deleting the App to remove the first two.

8. Children

SpokenAudio is a professional tool for adults (Terms, Section 1) and is not directed at children under 16. We knowingly collect no personal information from children; if we learn we have received some, we delete it.

9. Changes

We’ll post any change here with a new version and date, and show material changes in the App.

10. Our website

Our website, spokenaudio.com, is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub automatically logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, under GitHub’s Privacy Statement; in the EU and UK, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Because we don’t track you, we don’t respond differently to Do Not Track signals.
