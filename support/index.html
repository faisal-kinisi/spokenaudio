Support

We're here to help.

Most answers are in the app's Help tab, and the same answers are on the FAQ. If yours isn't, email support@spokenaudio.com. We're a small team; please allow up to 5 business days.

Never send patient or client information to support — no recordings, transcripts, names or screenshots that show them. Describe the problem in general terms; the app log below is enough for most problems.

Quick start

  1. RecordTap REC. Your words appear as you speak. Tap REC again to pause, RESUME to carry on.
  2. Fix a partMove to where you need to overwrite or insert new words (wheel, skip buttons, or tap a word on the page). Choose OVR to record over it, or INS to insert new words, then tap REC. Undo takes it back.
  3. SaveTap STOP, then Save. Your recording is in Sessions; its words are in Words.

Send the app log

If something goes wrong, the app log helps us find it. It holds times, numbers and settings — never your words, recording names or device names.

  1. Open Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ Share app logChoose the last 24 hours, or everything kept (about two weeks).
  2. Send it by emailto support@spokenaudio.com, with a short description of what happened and roughly when. Nothing is sent unless you share it.

Common questions

A recording stopped unexpectedly. Open SpokenAudio again: it asks Restore, or Keep as draft. The audio recorded up to that moment is kept either way. For a draft that won't open, Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ Export draft audio copies its audio out.

No words appear. The first time, iOS downloads its speech model; keep the iPhone online until it finishes. Check that SpokenAudio may use the microphone: iPhone Settings ▸ Apps ▸ SpokenAudio.

Purchase or trial. Restore purchase (Settings ▸ SpokenAudio, or the unlock screen) brings back an unlock bought with the same Apple Account. Apple handles payments and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Opening an export. The recipient needs the password and: current Microsoft Word for the .docx; Adobe Acrobat Reader, Preview on Mac, Chrome or the iPhone Files app for the PDF; 7-Zip on Windows, or Keka or The Unarchiver on Mac, for the .7z. A new password is shown once when you export.

A new iPhone. Recordings can't be opened on another iPhone, even from a backup — the key stays on this one. Export what you need to keep before you change iPhones.
