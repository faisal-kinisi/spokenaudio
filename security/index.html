Security and standards

SpokenAudio uses published, widely reviewed encryption standards and no secret methods of its own.

Recordings, transcripts, modification record on the iPhone

AES-256-GCM, a fresh nonce per block, via Apple CryptoKit. Standards: FIPS 197 (AES), NIST SP 800-38D (GCM).

The encryption key

A 256-bit random key in the iPhone’s Keychain, “after first unlock, this device only”. See Apple Platform Security.

Word export (.docx)

Office “Agile” encryption: AES-256-CBC, SHA-512 key derivation with 100,000 rounds, HMAC integrity check. Standards: MS-OFFCRYPTO, FIPS 180-4 (SHA-2), FIPS 198-1 (HMAC).

PDF export

PDF 2.0 standard security handler, revision 6: AES-256 (AESV3), a fresh random IV per stream, SHA-256/384/512 key derivation. Standard: ISO 32000-2.

Audio and text export (.7z)

7-Zip AES-256 with SHA-256 key derivation; file names inside encrypted. Standards: the 7z format, NIST SP 800-38A (CBC).

Export passwords

Made by the iPhone’s secure random-number generator. If you choose to save one for reuse, it is kept in the iPhone’s Keychain (this iPhone only) and Face ID or the passcode is needed to show it; otherwise it is shown once and not kept.

Audio checksum

SHA-256 of the recording as WAV — every WAV export of the same version of the recording gives the same checksum — plus, for an ALAC or M4A export, that file’s own SHA-256. With exported audio, a checksum file goes with it. Standard: FIPS 180-4.

HIPAA and healthcare use

SpokenAudio records, transcribes and stores your recordings and transcripts only on your iPhone. We never collect, receive, store or transmit them, so we have no access to Protected Health Information (PHI) and are not a Business Associate under HIPAA (HHS guidance on software vendors without access to PHI). A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is not needed to use the app.

SpokenAudio provides technical safeguards — on-device processing, AES-256 encryption on the iPhone and password-protected exports. HIPAA compliance depends on how your practice configures its devices and handles information; please check with your compliance officer about your organization’s mobile-device and data-export policies.

When you contact support, please don’t send recordings, transcripts or anything containing patient, client or other sensitive information.

What this does not cover

No software, device or encryption is completely secure. Your protection depends just as much on things only you control: your iPhone’s passcode and who can unlock it, where you send exported files, how you send their passwords, the file names you choose, and the security of the computers and services that receive them. Encryption standards are reviewed by the security community and may be updated as threats change; we will update the App when they are. SpokenAudio does not claim certification under FIPS 140, HIPAA, or any other program.
