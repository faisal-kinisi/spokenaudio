Security · the worst case, considered

Cloud dictation has servers to breach.
SpokenAudio stays on your iPhone.

SpokenAudio removes the whole chain: speech becomes text on your iPhone, and it is encrypted before it is saved. We never receive your recordings or your words.

Questions

The short answers.

Does it need the internet?

Only twice: once when iOS downloads its speech model, and to buy or restore. Dictating, editing and exporting work with no connection.

Can I use it for patient or client dictation?

Yes — it's built for confidential work: speech recognition on the iPhone, AES-256 encryption, Face ID, and we never receive your recordings or words. HIPAA compliance depends on how your practice handles information; check with your compliance officer.

What if my iPhone is lost?

A locked iPhone protects your recordings — use a strong passcode and turn on SpokenAudio's app lock.

Do I need special hardware?

No. The iPhone's microphone, AirPods or a Bluetooth headset — no foot pedal, no recorder, no transcription service.
